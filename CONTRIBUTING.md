# Contributing

Operational Estate improves by being argued with. The most useful contributions are:

- **A competency question it cannot answer.** Write it in the three parts
  [competency-questions.md](competency-questions.md) uses: what the estate returns, the last mile,
  and what makes the answer the same for everyone. A question that needs a term the model lacks is
  how a term gets proposed.
- **A fact from a real estate that has no home**, or that has to be forced in. The sentence "a
  Service too, if we need it" is the signal that the model is wrong somewhere.
- **A disagreement with a definition**, with the case that breaks it.

Open an issue for any of these before a pull request that changes the model, so the argument
happens where others can join it.

## Terms and changes

Every term is `draft` until the first published version, and a draft term may be renamed or
removed. After publication, a term is added and never removed or repurposed: one that turns out
wrong is deprecated with a pointer to its replacement.

A proposed term is stated against the problem, never against a product: its definition, what it is
not, its key, its domain and range, its direction and inverse, and the competency question it
serves.

## Sign-off

Contributions are accepted under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Sign off each commit to certify
the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/):

